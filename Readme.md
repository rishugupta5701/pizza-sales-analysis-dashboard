# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pizza sales data using Microsoft Power BI to identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of overall sales performance, customer ordering patterns, pizza category performance, and best- and worst-selling products.

The goal is to help business stakeholders make data-driven decisions to improve sales performance and product strategy.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall pizza sales performance.
- Identify daily and monthly ordering trends.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by pizza category.
- Understand sales distribution across pizza sizes.
- Identify the best- and worst-performing pizzas.
- Generate actionable business insights from sales data.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard consists of two interactive pages.

### 1. Sales Overview Dashboard

This page provides a high-level overview of pizza sales performance.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 817.86K |
| Total Orders | 21K |
| Total Pizzas Sold | 50K |
| Average Order Value | 38.31 |
| Average Pizzas Per Order | 2.32 |

**Visualizations:**
- Total Orders by Day of Week
- Total Orders by Month
- Revenue by Pizza Category
- Orders by Pizza Size
- Total Pizzas Sold by Category

### 2. Best & Worst Seller Dashboard

This page focuses on identifying the highest- and lowest-performing pizza products.

**Best Seller Analysis:**
- Top 5 Pizzas by Revenue
- Top 5 Pizzas by Quantity Sold
- Top 5 Pizzas by Total Orders

**Worst Seller Analysis:**
- Bottom 5 Pizzas by Revenue
- Bottom 5 Pizzas by Quantity Sold
- Bottom 5 Pizzas by Total Orders

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Busiest Days:** Friday and Saturday generate the highest number of orders.
2. **Busiest Months:** January and July are among the months with the highest order volumes.
3. **Top Category:** Classic pizza contributes the highest share of revenue.
4. **Pizza Size:** Large-size pizzas contribute the most to sales.
5. **Top Revenue Product:** Thai Chicken Pizza generates the highest revenue.
6. **Lowest Revenue Product:** Brie Carre Pizza generates the lowest revenue.
